# Demo script

Five takes. One screen each. Record them separately — nothing depends on the take before it.
Read the bold lines like you mean them; they are the ones that win this.

---

**1 · Title card**

> Agents that spend money get a funded key, or a permission popup you stop reading.
>
> **Neither one is consent.**

---

**2 · Claude Code — type the goal, talk over the finished table**

```
vet 3 counterparty wallets before I pay them
```

> The agent shopped first. Real sellers, real prices, quoted over HTTP just now.
>
> **A plan: what each step buys, why it earns its price, what it costs.** Nothing spent yet.

---

**3 · Your phone — press "Check these sellers on chain", then approve**

> Before I fund strangers: has anyone ever actually paid them? That's The Graph, reading each
> seller's own payout address.
>
> **Approving is the funding.** It creates a Hedera account holding exactly this ceiling. The
> agent can propose a payment from it. It can never complete one alone.

---

**4 · The blocked step, then the diff**

> A seller asked more than it quoted. The payment stopped — **with money still in the
> envelope.**
>
> It stopped because the plan I approved was not the plan the agent found. **That is the
> difference between a spending limit and an approved plan.**
>
> No popup. A diff, and three ways out, each priced.

---

**5 · The receipts**

> Funded. Paid. Swept back. Quoted equals paid.
>
> Everyone else gives the agent a funded wallet and enforces the limit in their own service.
>
> **We give the agent zero funds and let consensus enforce the cap.**

---

**If it breaks:** a dead seller is the product working — the agent swaps in another. A sweep
that doesn't fire is covered by the refund scheduled at mint. Say what happened and keep going.

**Read money as words.** "Five cents", not "zero point zero five".
