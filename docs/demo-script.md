# Demo script

Five takes. One screen each. Record them separately — nothing depends on the take before it.
Read the bold lines like you mean them; they are the ones that win this.

**How to record it** is at the bottom. Read that first if you have not set up yet.

---

**1 · Title card**

> Agents that spend money get a funded key, or a permission popup you stop reading.
>
> **Neither one is consent.**

---

**2 · Claude Code — type the goal, talk over the finished table**

```
vet 3 counterparty wallets before I pay them
```

> The agent shopped first. Real sellers, real prices, quoted over HTTP just now.
>
> **A plan: what each step buys, why it earns its price, what it costs.** Nothing spent yet.

---

**3 · Your phone — press "Check these sellers on chain", then approve**

> Before I fund strangers: has anyone ever actually paid them? That's The Graph, reading each
> seller's own payout address.
>
> **Approving is the funding.** It creates a Hedera account holding exactly this ceiling. The
> agent can propose a payment from it. It can never complete one alone.

---

**4 · The blocked step, then the diff**

> A seller asked more than it quoted. The payment stopped — **with money still in the
> envelope.**
>
> It stopped because the plan I approved was not the plan the agent found. **That is the
> difference between a spending limit and an approved plan.**
>
> No popup. A diff, and three ways out, each priced.

---

**5 · The receipts**

> Funded. Paid. Swept back. Quoted equals paid.
>
> Everyone else gives the agent a funded wallet and enforces the limit in their own service.
>
> **We give the agent zero funds and let consensus enforce the cap.**

---

**If it breaks:** a dead seller is the product working — the agent swaps in another. A sweep
that doesn't fire is covered by the refund scheduled at mint. Say what happened and keep going.

**Read money as words.** "Five cents", not "zero point zero five".

---

## How to record it

**Use QuickTime. It is already on the Mac and it will not fight you.**

`File → New Screen Recording`. Before you press record:

1. **Options → Microphone → MacBook Pro Microphone.** Without this you get a silent video, and
   it is the single most common way this goes wrong.
2. **Options → Show Mouse Clicks.** Judges need to see where you pressed.
3. Record **one window**, not the whole desktop — drag a box around the terminal, or around
   the browser. Nobody needs your dock or your other tabs.

Then record the five takes into five files. QuickTime saves each one when you stop.

### Your face

**Do it as a separate clip, not a floating bubble.** Record one 15-second piece to camera
first — QuickTime `File → New Movie Recording` — saying take 1 out loud. That becomes your
opening. Everything after it is screen only, with your voice over it.

Why: a picture-in-picture bubble is fiddly to place, covers something on every screen, and if
it goes wrong you lose the whole take. A talking-head opener plus clean screen recordings is
faster to shoot and reads more confident.

If you would rather not appear on camera at all, that is fine — voice over the screen is
enough. **Do not skip the voice.**

### Stitching it together

Open **iMovie**, drag the six clips in, in order, trim the dead air at the front and back of
each. Export at 1080p. That is the whole edit — no titles, no music, no transitions.

### Before you press record

- Silence notifications: `Focus → Do Not Disturb`.
- Have the approval link already open on your phone, and your phone already unlocked.
- Zoom the terminal up two sizes. It will be watched in a small window.
- Say the take number out loud at the start of each clip. It makes them trivial to order later.

### If you run out of time

**Record take 3 and take 4 first.** The approval page and the blocked step are the product.
Everything else can be cut and the video still makes the argument.
